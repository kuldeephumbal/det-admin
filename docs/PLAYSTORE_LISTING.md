## Full description (4000 chars)

```
DET is a clean, privacy-first expense tracker for people who actually
want to know where their money goes.

Log a purchase in under three taps, set monthly budgets, get reminded
before a bill is due, and share accounts with family so everyone sees
the same view. No ads, no data brokers, no upsells in the workflow —
the free tier covers everything you need to stay on top of your
spending.

— Why DET —

• ONE-TAP EXPENSE ENTRY. The big FAB on Home and Expenses opens a
  bottom sheet — pick a category, type an amount, hit save. We pre-fill
  the category based on your past spending so common notes ("Uber",
  "rent", "groceries") auto-select the right bucket.

• BUDGETS THAT WARN YOU EARLY. Set overall or per-category limits,
  monthly or yearly. The dashboard ring fills as you spend; an alert
  fires when you cross 80%, 100%, and 120%.

• BILLS YOU WON'T MISS. Add recurring or one-off bills with due dates.
  We push a reminder three days before, one day before, and on the day
  of. Mark paid in one tap — it auto-creates the matching expense.

• ACCOUNTS / WALLETS. Track cash, bank, credit card, UPI, savings —
  each with its own balance. Transfer between them; balances stay in
  sync.

• DEBTS LEDGER. Track who you lent money to and who you borrowed from.
  Log partial repayments. Mark settled when it's done.

• SPENDING CALENDAR. Month grid heatmap — pick any day to see every
  expense logged on it.

• ANALYTICS. Real charts, not pie-chart fluff. Daily / weekly /
  monthly / yearly trend lines, top categories, where the money goes.

— Premium features —

• AI INSIGHTS. Weekly summaries that highlight anomalies — "you spent
  3× your usual on food this week", "subscriptions up ₹2,000 vs last
  month". Narrated by a real LLM, not templated.

• CASH-FLOW FORECAST. A 30-day forward projection of your balance
  composing recurring expenses, upcoming bills, average discretionary
  spending, and savings auto-contributions. Warns when you're heading
  for a dip below zero.

• FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE. A 0–100 monthly score with the four
  components that drive it — saving rate, debt load, spending
  volatility, budget adherence — so you know exactly what to work on.

• SHARED ACCOUNTS. Invite your partner or family by email — they see
  every expense booked on the account, budgets apply to the pool. The
  owner manages who has access; anyone can leave any time.

• HOME-SCREEN WIDGET. Net worth + today's spending + one-tap add,
  right on your home screen.

— Privacy you can actually verify —

• No ads. No data brokers. We don't sell your data, period.
• Admins of the DET service can NEVER see your individual financial
  data — by design. Read the privacy policy at det.app/privacy.
• Open about every third-party integration: Google for sign-in,
  Firebase for push, Anthropic for AI insights, optional Plaid / Setu
  for bank sync. Each one is opt-in.

— Free vs Premium —

Free includes everything you need for daily tracking: unlimited
expenses, budgets, accounts, bills, debts, categories, calendar, and
analytics.

Premium unlocks the AI Insights, cash-flow forecast, financial health
score, shared accounts, and the home-screen widget. ₹199 / month or
₹1,499 / year. Cancel any time from your Play Store subscriptions.

— Built by an indie team —

DET is built by a small team that uses it every day. We read every
support email at support@det.app and ship updates regularly. If
something's broken or missing, tell us — we usually reply within a
day.
```
